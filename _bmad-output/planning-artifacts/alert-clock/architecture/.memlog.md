- (decision) AD-1: A same-minute alert or an alert that becomes due during an active alert joins one combined occurrence. App owns one active occurrence; Stop and auto-stop resolve all joined alerts, while Postpone defers all joined alerts to one shared due time. Binds FR-4, FR-7, FR-8, FR-9; prevents competing active states, buzzer ownership, and acknowledgement semantics.
- (decision) AD-2: Persist pending postponed occurrence. On startup, restore only if its local due time has not passed; discard an overdue occurrence without ringing. Binds FR-8 and NFR-3; prevents a late unexpected alarm after power restoration.
- (constraint) Inherited: functional-core-imperative-shell, App single-writer state ownership, monotonic duration scheduling, DisplayPort-only renderers, and Wi-Fi SettingsStore ownership from core/wifi/touch architecture spines bind alert-clock scope.
- (constraint) Hardware blocker: no buzzer GPIO assignment or driver activation before actual HW-508 pin order, 3.3 V compatibility, GPIO current, and safe drive method are bench-verified and docs/hardware_configuration.md is updated.
- (decision) AD-3: Configuration save is transactional for future scheduling only. An already-raised active or postponed occurrence retains captured alert identities and completes through Stop, Postpone, or auto-stop; web edits never cancel it. Binds FR-1, FR-4, FR-7, FR-8; prevents web mutation racing active touch and buzzer resolution.
- (decision) AD-4: Global postpone delay is persisted whole minutes, validated inclusively from 1 through 60, default 10. Binds FR-3 and FR-8; prevents web validation, scheduler calculation, and TFT labels diverging.
- (decision) AD-5: Scheduler uses local civil time and identity key for due detection/duplicate suppression; App uses wrap-safe monotonic ticks for auto-stop and sound cadence. Prevents wall-time corrections corrupting elapsed behavior.
- (decision) AD-6: Active alert is exclusive App-owned surface. It preempts Rotation, Bar, Settings, and their deadlines; pure debounced hit testing emits Stop or Postpone. Prevents stale normal-screen input and rendering during an alert.
- (decision) AD-7: BuzzerPort is imperative shell with bounded per-loop tick and immediate silence. It cannot be activated or assigned GPIO until HW-508 electrical bench verification. Prevents blocking sound and unverified drive.
- (decision) AD-8: Buzzer/render failures return named secret-free result codes; App logs , displays bounded failure state, and remains recoverable for later alerts. Prevents alert failures terminating cooperative operation.
