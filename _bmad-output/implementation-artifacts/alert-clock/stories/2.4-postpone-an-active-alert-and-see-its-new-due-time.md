<intent-contract>

## Intent

**Problem:** An active alert needs a safe way to defer its current occurrence without losing the captured alert membership or making the owner guess when it will return.

**Approach:** Add a debounced POSTPONE action that silences the occurrence, schedules one in-memory future due time using the configured delay, and shows an explicit confirmation with that due time before normal rotation resumes.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** Use configured postpone delay; preserve captured alert members and repeating/one-time semantics until the postponed occurrence resolves; silence before returning to normal rotation; show exact local due date/time; keep pure alert logic free of hardware imports; keep active touch handling exclusive and release-latched.

**Never:** Persist postponed state across reboot (Story 2.5); merge collisions (Story 2.6); apply live configuration changes to an active/postponed occurrence (Story 2.7); alter buzzer cadence or auto-stop behavior beyond this action.

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Expected Output / Behavior | Error Handling |
|----------|--------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| HAPPY_PATH | Active occurrence; debounced edge in lower POSTPONE band; delay 10 minutes | Silence, store due local date/time, render explicit confirmation, resume rotation | No error expected |
| REALERT | Postponed occurrence reaches due local minute | Recreate active alert from captured members and sound it once | Do not retrofire before due time |
| BOUNCE | Held/replayed edge before release | One postpone only; no normal-surface navigation | Consume until release |
| INVALID_DELAY | Missing, non-positive, or malformed configured delay | Use bounded configured/default behavior without crash or negative due time | Keep loop recoverable and log secret-free failure if needed |

</intent-contract>

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given an active occurrence and configured delay, when a valid debounced touch lands in POSTPONE band, then buzzer silence occurs before any later tick and the UI confirms the exact new local due time.
- Given a postponed occurrence before its due time, when App loops, then normal rotation continues and the occurrence does not re-alert early or repeatedly.
- Given a postponed occurrence at or after its due local minute, when App loops, then the captured occurrence becomes active again and buzzer cadence resumes once.
- Given repeating members, when POSTPONE is selected, then they remain enabled and retain their captured occurrence membership.
- Given one-time members, when the postponed occurrence finally resolves, then existing canonical ids are disabled using current terminal behavior; missing ids do not recreate settings.
- Given bounce or replayed edges, when App loops before release, then POSTPONE runs once and cannot open normal navigation.

## Design Notes

Postpone is an occurrence transition, not an alert-setting edit. Due state must remain attached to the captured occurrence so later configuration changes or missing one-time ids cannot silently change what is being resolved. Confirmation may be transient, but its displayed local due date/time must be derived from the same normalized due value used by re-alert logic.
